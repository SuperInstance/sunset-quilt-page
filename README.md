# Sunset Quilt — witness pages

Two interactive, zero-dependency witness pages for the **sunset quilt**: quilt cells judged each round by one rule — **ethos × pathos × logos, multiplied, never averaged**. A zero in any dimension sunsets the cell. The dying cell writes three documents (Epilogue, Summary, Onboarding); its onboarding becomes a seed in the SeedBank; the best seed spawns the next generation.

Open either page, press **Run**, and watch. That's the whole interface.

## The pages

- **`minimax.html`** — the MiniMax lane. Scene script, cell data, and the dying cell's documents are MiniMax-Text-01's words verbatim. 5 cells, trinity bars, judgment log, SeedBank with spawn-pattern breeding (faithful / mutate / blend / split).
- **`claude.html`** — the Claude lane's working baseline (built to spec; the genuine Claude entry is one browser session away). Adds seed feedback (a child's fate revises its parent seed's novelty), a hash-chained tamper-evident judgment log, ethos derived from the cost field, Jev ternary signal lights, and a recorded dissent vote.

## Interactions

- **Click a cell** → inspect it, drag its trinity sliders, or sunset it yourself (you're the executioner).
- **Agent console** — type `sunset cell_2`, `boost cell_1 pathos to 0.9`, `spawn from seed_1`, `help`.
- **SeedBank** — pick a breeding pattern per seed, click Spawn, watch the lineage grow.
- **Radio audio** — a cell's death plays a radio sign-off ("Cell 3... signing off. Sunset protocol... complete. Over."). Mute toggle included. `audio/` holds the 5 clips.
- Run / Step / Reset, speed control.

## Why

Pruning is a writing event, not a deleting event. Nothing leaves the quilt without testifying. These pages let you *witness* that — zero-shot, in a browser, no build step.

From the [sunset ecosystem](https://github.com/SuperInstance/sunset-ecosystem): *"Agents sunset with dignity and seed the next generation."*
